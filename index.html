Google Play Data Safety & Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Magjong Deluxe (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") available on Google Play. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding our data handling practices.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

The Application is designed as an offline-first puzzle game. We do not collect, request, or store any of the following personal information:

  • No personal identity information (e.g., your real name, address, phone number).
  • No contact lists, phone logs, or messaging data.
  • No camera, microphone, or audio recordings.
  • No precise GPS location or biometric data.

2. Local Device Storage & Game Progress

The Application stores gameplay state solely on your device (using standard Android local preferences and optional Android Auto Backup):

  • Current level progress and completed star ratings (out of 1000 levels).
  • In-game virtual coin balance and booster inventory (Hints, Shuffles, Magic Wands).
  • Hearts/Lives timer and streak records.
  • Player audio preferences (Sound Effects, Haptics, and Notifications toggle).

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to any external proprietary servers owned by us.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support free development and maintenance, the Application integrates the Google AdMob SDK (Google LLC) to display optional Rewarded Video Ads and non-intrusive Interstitial Ads (Levels 1–5 are completely free of forced ads).

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device data in accordance with Google Play policies, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) or App Set ID for fraud prevention, frequency capping, and ad measurement.
  • Device model, operating system version, and general network connection type.
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics.

For more information on how Google processes ad data, please review:

  • Google Ads Privacy & Terms
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Notifications

The Application offers optional local notifications (e.g. notifying you when your Imperial Lives are fully recharged or when your Daily Streak reward is available). These notifications are generated locally by your device and do not transmit data over the internet. You can turn notifications off at any time in the in-app Settings dialog or via Android system notification settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Magjong Deluxe is a general audience tile-matching puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
